# ind-c — Hoteller, restauranter og køkkener 2025–2028

Start 1 March 2025 (§ 30, stk. 1). Rates carry on after expiry (§ 30, stk. 2).

## Ambiguities and choices

1. **Holiday allowance changes on 1 September 2026 (§ 18, stk. 2).** This is feriegodtgørelse: 12.5 %, then 12.75 % "for løn, der optjenes fra og med 1. september 2026". It splits on a date that matches no other change. The engine pays a shift by the day it starts, which fits "løn der optjenes". The engine percentage is 12.5 and then 12.75. The 12.75 % is above the statutory 12.5 %, as the agreement may set.
2. **Minimum wage rises given as kr. amounts (§ 11, stk. 2).** "De tre satser ... forhøjes ... med 3,60 kr. ... og ... med yderligere 3,40 kr." The rates come to 131.75 / 135.40 / 139.10, then 135.35 / 139.00 / 142.70, then 138.75 / 142.40 / 146.10. Bands are at 0, 9 ("efter ni måneders beskæftigelse") and 36 ("efter tre års"). "Beskæftigelse" is read as the same as employment seniority.
3. **Pension from 1 March 2026 (§ 16, stk. 2).** The employer's share goes up by 1 point and the employee's down by 1 point, so 9.0 / 3.0 with the 12.0 total unchanged. Qualifying period is 12 months.
4. **Daily norm, § 8 stk. 1: 8 hours,** even though the week averages 37 hours over 4 weeks (§ 4). The engine only does a daily norm, and the agreement says so explicitly ("også når ugens samlede timetal ikke overstiger 37 timer").
5. **Sunday 25 %, then 30 % from 2027-03-01 (§ 10, stk. 1).** Overtime tiers are 2 h at 50 %, 2 h at 75 %, then 100 %.

## Not expressible (left out)

- § 10 stk. 3 helligdagstillæg (60 kr. per hour on a public holiday that is not a Sunday) and § 11 stk. 4 afløsertillæg (11.25 kr. per hour, only for afløsere).
- § 4 stk. 3 minimum 4 hours paid, § 5 stk. 2, § 7 delt tjeneste (48 kr. per day), § 8 stk. 3 varslingstillæg, § 9 stk. 3 night porters' monthly supplement, § 12 fagtillæg and stedfortrædertillæg.
- Apprentices get half the § 9 supplements (Bilag 1, stk. 2), and under-18s have their own rate. Both are special groups.

## Possible mistakes in the agreement

- None found. All arithmetic comes out exact to the øre.
