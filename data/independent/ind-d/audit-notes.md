# ind-d — Bagerier og konditorier 2025–2028

Start 1 April 2025 (§ 27, stk. 1). Rates carry on (§ 27, stk. 2).

## Ambiguities and choices

1. **Inconvenient-hours rates change on 1 October 2026 (§ 6, stk. 1).** The other rates change on 1 April. The split is at 2026-10-01, and no change happens on 1 April 2026 or 2027.
2. **"Til og med 31. marts" (§ 9, stk. 1 and 2)** gives until = 1 April. Bands are at 0 ("nyansatte"), 3 ("tre måneders"), 12 ("et års") and 60 ("fem års").
3. **Søgnehelligdagskonto 3.5 % (§ 16, stk. 1) is left out of special_savings.** § 15 stk. 3 says it "er ikke en del af fritvalgskontoen og kommer oven i". A key could defend 7.2 + 3.5 = 10.7 % as the employer's total savings percentage. I chose 7.2 / 8.2 / 9.0, the fritvalg figure only, because the kind is defined as fritvalg or særlig opsparing.
4. **Holiday allowance, § 17 stk. 2: 13.0.** This is feriegodtgørelse ("Feriegodtgørelsen ... er 13,0 pct."), above the statutory 12.5 %. The engine percentage is 13.0.
5. **Overtime tiers, § 7 stk. 2:** 1 h at 50 %, 2 h at 75 % (the second and third hour), then 100 %. Sunday is 65 % in place of overtime (§ 7, stk. 3).
6. **Inconvenient-hours windows.** These are Mon–Sat 00–04, Mon–Sat 04–06, Mon–Fri 18–24 and Sun 00–06. Saturday and Sunday evenings carry no supplement. The time on Monday 00–06 after a Sunday evening falls on Monday and gets the Monday rates, which is what the table says.

## Not expressible (left out)

- § 16 stk. 3: 80 % for work on a public holiday. The søgnehelligdagskonto (3.5 %, with 1,100 kr. advances) is also left out.
- § 4 stk. 3 (70 kr. per day for a moved start time), § 7 stk. 4 (85 kr.), § 8 rådighedsvagt, § 10 fag-, ansvars- and smudstillæg, § 23 fund (0.45 kr. per hour).
- Under-18s (60 % or 70 % of the new-hire rate) and apprentices (60 % of genetillæg) are special groups.

## Possible mistakes in the agreement

- § 14 stk. 1: "ansat i tre måneder" sets qualifying months at 3. No conflict.
- None found otherwise.
