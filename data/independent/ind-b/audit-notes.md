# ind-b — Rengørings- og serviceoverenskomsten 2025–2027

Start 1 May 2025 (§ 27, stk. 1). The last rates carry on (§ 27, stk. 2).

## Ambiguities and choices

1. **Sunday supplement changes on 1 January 2026 (Protokollat 1).** § 8 stk. 3 gives 75 %, and Protokollat 1 raises it to 85 % from 2026-01-01. So the overtime rule splits on 2026-01-01, a date that matches no other rate change. A key that keeps 75 % throughout, or moves the change to 1 May 2026, would be wrong.
2. **Seniority supplements (Bilag 1, stk. 1).** "Efter 6 måneder ... 3,25 kr." and "Efter 2 års ansættelse ... yderligere 2,50 kr. ..., så det samlede anciennitetstillæg er 5,75 kr." These become bands at 0, 6 and 24 months: 134.20 / 137.45 / 139.95, then 138.00 / 141.25 / 143.75 from 2026-05-01. The supplements stay fixed while the base rises.
3. **Pension split (§ 14, stk. 2).** 13.2 % in total, with the employer paying two thirds and the employee one third. That gives 8.80 / 4.40, and it comes out exact. Qualifying period is 6 months ("ansat i seks måneder").
4. **Special savings from 1 May 2026.** 6.4 % "forhøjes med 1,0 procentpoint", so 7.4 %.
5. **Inconvenient hours, "Der betales kun ét tillæg ... for samme time".** The text does not say which supplement is paid when two apply. The engine pays the highest. In this table no two windows overlap, so the choice changes nothing. Sunday 00:00–05:00 falls only in the "alle ugens dage 00–05" row, and the Sunday weekend rate starts at 05:00.
6. **Holiday allowance, § 17 stk. 2: 12.5.** This is feriegodtgørelse ("Feriegodtgørelsen udgør 12,5 pct."), not ferietillæg. The engine percentage is 12.5.

## Not expressible (left out)

- § 16 stk. 2: work on a public holiday is paid as Sunday work. The engine has no holidays.
- § 4 stk. 3 delt arbejdstid (37.50 kr. per day), § 10 task supplements, § 13 tøjgodtgørelse (2.10 kr. per hour, conditional), § 14 stk. 3 barsel pension in kr. per hour, § 24 fund.
- Under-18 rates and apprentices (Bilag 2) are special groups.

## Possible mistakes in the agreement

- § 7 stk. 2 says supplements are "beregnes pr. påbegyndt minut". The engine pays whole minutes worked, which is the same thing for whole-minute shifts. This is not a mistake.
- § 8 stk. 1 limits overtime to work "efter virksomhedens ordre". The engine cannot tell ordered hours from unordered ones.
