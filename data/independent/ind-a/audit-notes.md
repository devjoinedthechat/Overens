# ind-a — Lager- og terminaloverenskomsten 2025–2028

Start 1 March 2025 (§ 30, stk. 1). The last rates carry on after the agreement ends (§ 30, stk. 2), so every kind ends open.

## Ambiguities and choices

1. **Holiday allowance, § 17 stk. 2: 12.5.** The clause says "feriegodtgørelse med 12,5 pct." This is feriegodtgørelse, the statutory holiday pay for an employee who is not paid during holiday. It is not ferietillæg. The engine's single percentage is 12.5. No ferietillæg is stated.
2. **Supplement rates after 1 March 2027 (§ 6, stk. 2).** "De forhøjede satser gælder også efter 1. marts 2027." I read this as no further rise, so one rule from 2026-03-01 with no end. The sentence only confirms the 2026 rates; it adds no new step.
3. **Night window, § 6 stk. 1, "uanset ugedag".** I took this as all seven days, Sunday included: 22:00–06:00, wrapping. On Saturday 22:00–24:00 both the night rate (41.75/43.00) and the Saturday rate (52.10/53.65) apply. § 6 stk. 3 says only the highest is paid, which matches the engine's "highest window wins". On Sunday 00:00–06:00 only the night rate applies, because the Saturday window ends at 00:00.
4. **Pension qualifying period (§ 14, stk. 1): 2 months.** "Ansat i virksomheden i to måneder" is read as 2 full months. The pension base is "skattepligtige løn". The engine takes pay including supplements, and I treated these as the same.
5. **Minimum wage bands.** "Under 12 måneder", "Fra 12 måneder" and "Fra 36 måneder" become bands at 0, 12 and 36. Under-18s (§ 11 stk. 1), apprentices and adult apprentices are left out as special groups.

## Not expressible in the six kinds (left out)

- § 9 stk. 1: a 100 % helligdagstillæg for public holidays on a weekday. The engine knows no holidays.
- § 4 stk. 2 omlægningstillæg (165 kr. per changed shift), § 7 holddriftstillæg (310 kr. per week), § 8 stk. 5 tilkald (240 kr. plus at least 3 hours), § 12 kulde- and trucktillæg (per hour, but tied to the task, not the time of day).
- § 15 stk. 2: health scheme at 0.15 % of pay. This is not fritvalg or særlig opsparing, so it is not special_savings.
- § 24 stk. 2: the competence fund at 780 kr. per year.

## Possible mistakes in the agreement

- None found. Every figure is stated outright, so no arithmetic was needed.
