# uns-b — Vagt- og sikkerhedsfunktionærer 2025–2027

Start 1 October 2025 (§ 26, stk. 1). Rates carry on (§ 26, stk. 3).

## Ambiguities and choices

1. **Holiday allowance, § 17 stk. 2: 1.5, then 2.0 from 2026-10-01.** This is the most consequential choice. The agreement states only ferietillæg: 1.5 % "indtil 30. september 2026", then 2 %. I used the stated figure. The same alternative applies as in uns-a. Vagtfunktionærer are paid by the hour (§ 7, stk. 1), and under ferieloven an hourly-paid employee without løn under ferie gets 12.5 % feriegodtgørelse. The agreement does not say that figure, so it is not in the rule. The figure is ferietillæg, not feriegodtgørelse.
2. **Minimum wage built from increments (§ 7, stk. 2–4, Protokollat 1).**
   - Bands are at 0, 6, 18 and 48 months.
   - From 2025-10-01: 161.20, 165.95, 169.05, 174.45.
   - From 2026-10-01: the starting wage rises by 4.35, and the increments are added to it. That gives 165.55, 170.30, 173.40, 178.80.
   - From 2027-04-01: the 48-month band only rises another 2.00, to 180.80. Protokollat 1 stk. 2 says the other bands are unchanged.
   - That makes three minimum-wage periods, the last starting on a date (1 April 2027) that is not an anniversary of the agreement.
3. **Night supplement, § 11 stk. 2.** "udgør 160 pct. af aftentillægget" means 1.6 × 15.85 = 25.36, and 1.6 × 16.40 = 26.24 from 2026-10-01. I did not read it as "forhøjet med 160 pct." (2.6 ×). The weekend supplement is aftentillæg + 3.15: 19.00, then 19.55. § 11 stk. 4 says both follow the raised aftentillæg.
4. **Windows.**
   - Night is all days 23:00–07:00 and wraps. Each hour after midnight counts on its own weekday, and because the window covers all seven days, wrapping is exact.
   - Weekend is Sat and Sun 07:00–23:00.
   - Evening is Mon–Fri 17:00–23:00.
   - No two windows overlap.
5. **Overtime.** The norm is 8 hours. Tiers are 1 h at 25 %, 2 h at 50 % (the second and third), then 100 %. Sunday is 70 %, then 75 % from 2026-10-01 ("forhøjes ... med 5 procentpoint").
6. **Pension (§ 15).** 12.6 % in total with the employee paying 4.2, so the employer pays 8.4. From 2026-10-01 the total is 13.1 % and the whole rise falls on the employer: 8.9 / 4.2. Qualifying period is 3 months.
7. **Special savings, § 18 and Protokollat 2:** 4 %, then 5 % from 2026-10-01, then 5.5 % from 2027-04-01.

## Not expressible (left out)

- § 5 stk. 3 ulempegodtgørelse (185 kr.), § 8 hundefører-, funktions- and førstehjælpstillæg, § 12 rådighed (38.50 kr. per hour, not working time), § 13 kørsel, § 24 fund.
- Under-18s (70 % of the starting wage) and elever (118.75 kr.) are special groups.

## Possible mistakes in the agreement

- None found. Every derived figure is exact to the øre.
