# uns-d — Industrivaskerier 2026–2028

Start 1 September 2026 (§ 25, stk. 1). The 1 September 2027 rates carry on (§ 25, stk. 3).

## Ambiguities and choices

1. **Holiday allowance, § 15 stk. 2: 2.0.** The agreement states ferietillæg 2 % only. I used the stated figure. The alternative is 12.5 % statutory feriegodtgørelse for hourly-paid staff, which the agreement does not state; see the uns-a note. The figure is ferietillæg, not feriegodtgørelse.
2. **Late and night windows (§ 10, stk. 1–2).** This is the trap in this agreement. The table gives Mon–Fri 23:00–24:00 and **Tue–Sat** 00:00–06:00 at the same rate, and stk. 2 confirms that Monday before 06:00 and Sunday get nothing.
   - The engine counts hours after midnight on the weekday they fall on. A single wrapping window "Mon–Fri 23:00–06:00" would therefore wrongly pay Monday 00–06 and miss Saturday 00–06.
   - I wrote two windows: Mon–Fri 23:00–00:00 and Tue–Sat 00:00–06:00.
   - Saturday 06:00–24:00 is 24.30 / 25.10. That includes Saturday 23–24, which is lower than the night rate, and the table states it that way. Saturday night into Sunday 00–06 gets nothing.
3. **Overtime tiers change on 1 March 2027 (Protokollat 1).** Before that date: 2 h at 50 %, 1 h at 75 %, then 100 %. From it, the 3rd hour moves to 100 %: 2 h at 50 %, then 100 %. This date matches no other change. Sunday 100 % and norm 7.4 h are unchanged.
4. **Pension employee share (§ 13, stk. 2).** "12 pct. ..., hvoraf arbejdsgiveren betaler 8 pct." The employee's 4 % is implied as the remainder and is not stated outright. I used 8 / 4 with a 2-month qualifying period.
5. **Søgnehelligdagskonto 2.5 % (§ 16, stk. 2) is left out of special_savings.** It "er ikke en del af fritvalgslønkontoen". Special savings is 7 %, then 8 % from 2027-09-01. A key using 9.5 / 10.5 would have folded the holiday account in.
6. **Minimum wage bands:** 0 ("under 3 måneder"), 3 and 12, changing on 2027-09-01.

## Not expressible (left out)

- § 5 stk. 3 shift-change allowance (210 kr.), § 8 stk. 2 instruktørtillæg, § 9 stk. 4 meal allowance (75 kr.), § 22 fund (0.45 kr. per hour), and the søgnehelligdagskonto.
- Under-18s (101.60 kr. / 104.50 kr.) and elever are special groups.

## Possible mistakes in the agreement

- None found. The figures are stated outright.
