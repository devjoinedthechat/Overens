# uns-c — Hjemmepleje og praktisk hjælp 2026–2029

Start 1 May 2026 (§ 28, stk. 1). Rates carry on (§ 28, stk. 3).

## Ambiguities and choices

1. **Night supplement after 1 May 2027 (§ 12, stk. 2 and 5).** This is the most consequential choice among the rates.
   - § 12 stk. 2 defines nattillæg as "aftentillægget forhøjet med 50 pct.", and § 12 stk. 5 raises aftentillæg to 23.10 from 2027-05-01. It says lørdagstillæg stays at 18.20 "som hidtil", but it says nothing about nattillæg.
   - I chose 34.65 = 1.5 × 23.10. The supplement is defined by its link to aftentillæg, and only lørdagstillæg is held still by name.
   - The other reading keeps the night supplement at 33.45 (1.5 × 22.30).
2. **Holiday allowance, § 17 stk. 2: 1.5.** The agreement states ferietillæg 1.5 % only, unchanged for the whole period. I used the stated figure. The alternative is 12.5 % statutory feriegodtgørelse for hourly-paid staff, which the agreement does not state; see the uns-a note. The figure is ferietillæg, not feriegodtgørelse.
3. **Minimum wage from 1 May 2028 (Bilag 1, stk. 2).**
   - Trin 1 and trin 2 rise 2 %: 170.00 → 173.40 and 176.50 → 180.03.
   - Trin 3 rises by the same kr. amount as trin 2, which is 3.53: 181.20 → 184.73.
   - All are exact. Bands are at 0 (trin 1, "indtil ét års"), 12 (trin 2) and 48 (trin 3, "fra fire års").
4. **Daily norm (§ 11, stk. 1)** is one fifth of 37 hours, which is 7.4 h or 7 h 24 min. That is a whole number of minutes.
5. **Sunday from 1 November 2027 (Protokollat 2).** "halvanden gang den procentsats, der gjaldt" gives 1.5 × 50 = 75 %. The overtime rule splits on 2027-11-01.
6. **Night windows (§ 12, stk. 2).** Mon–Sat 00:00–07:00 and Mon–Sat 23:00–24:00 are written as two windows, not one wrapping 23–07 window. Supplements follow the weekday the hour falls on (§ 12, stk. 4), and no supplement is paid on Sundays.
   - Saturday 23–24 is covered. Sunday 00–07 (Saturday night continued) and Sunday 23–24 are not. Monday 00–07 is covered.
   - A single wrapping window would give the same result here. I kept two windows for clarity.
7. **Pension (§ 15).** 13.85 % in total, with the employee paying 4.1 and the employer the rest: 9.75 / 4.1. The qualifying period is "et halvt år", which is 6 months. From 2028-05-01 it is 3 months (Protokollat 3), with the rates unchanged. That makes two pension periods.
8. **Special savings (Bilag 2):** 6.75 % from 2026-05-01 to 2027-10-31, 7.5 % to 2028-04-30, then 8.25 %. "1. maj 2026 – 31. oktober 2027" is inclusive, so until = 2027-11-01.

## Not expressible (left out)

- § 5 stk. 2 laundry allowance (9.50 kr. per shift), § 6 kørsel and cycle, § 7 stk. 3 uddannelsestillæg (7.20 kr. per hour, qualification-bound), § 10 delt tjeneste and ulempegodtgørelse, § 13 tilkaldevagt (a third of the hourly wage), § 25 fund.
- Under-18s (112.30 kr.) are a special group.

## Possible mistakes in the agreement

- None found. All arithmetic comes out exact.
