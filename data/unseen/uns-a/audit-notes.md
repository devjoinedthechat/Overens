# uns-a — Chauffører i vognmands- og distributionsvirksomhed 2026–2029

Start 1 March 2026 (§ 26, stk. 1). The last rates carry on (§ 26, stk. 3).

## Ambiguities and choices

1. **Holiday allowance, § 16 stk. 2: 1.95.** This is the most consequential choice. The agreement states only ferietillæg: "Ferietillægget efter ferieloven udgør 1 pct." plus "et supplerende ferietillæg på 0,95 pct. af samme løn", paid together. I chose 1 + 0.95 = 1.95, the full percentage of pay the agreement makes payable as ferietillæg.
   - Alternative (a) is 0.95. That counts only what the agreement adds, because the 1 % is statutory and only restated.
   - Alternative (b) is 12.5 or 13.45. Chauffører are paid by the hour (§ 6), and an hourly-paid employee without løn under ferie gets 12.5 % feriegodtgørelse under ferieloven, not 1 % ferietillæg. The agreement never says that figure, so I did not use it.
   - The figure is ferietillæg, not feriegodtgørelse.
2. **Seniority supplement after three years (§ 6, stk. 2).** "Efter ni måneders ansættelse udgør anciennitetstillægget 3,80 kr. ... Efter tre års ansættelse udgør anciennitetstillægget 7,15 kr." I read "udgør" as the total supplement: 7.15 replaces 3.80 and is not added to it.
   - The bands are base, base + 3.80 and base + 7.15, at 0, 9 and 36 months.
   - The other reading is cumulative: base + 10.95 from 36 months.
   - § 6 stk. 3 says "grundlønnen ... med tillæg af det anciennitetstillæg, chaufføren har ret til", which fits the total reading.
3. **Base rates (Bilag 1).** 168.35, then +5.25 = 173.60 on 2027-03-01, then +5.35 = 178.95 on 2028-03-01. Minimum wage = base + seniority supplement, as § 6 stk. 3 and Bilag 1 stk. 3 say.
4. **Inconvenient-hours rises (§ 10, stk. 4).** On 2027-03-01 every rate goes up 2.5 %: 24.00→24.60, 31.20→31.98 and 28.80→29.52, all exact. On 2028-03-01 each rises by 0.75 kr.: 25.35, 32.73 and 30.27. There is no Sunday supplement (§ 10, stk. 2), and Sunday 00–06 is not in the Mon–Sat night row.
5. **Overtime tiers change on 2028-01-01 (Protokollat 2).** Before that date: 3 h at 50 %, then 100 %. From it: 2 h at 50 %, then 100 %. Norm 7 h 24 min and Sunday 100 % are unchanged.
6. **Pension (§ 14 and Protokollat 3).** 12 %, split two thirds / one third, gives 8.00 / 4.00. From 2027-07-01 the employer pays 8.6 and the employee still 4.0. Qualifying period is 2 months.
7. **Special savings, § 17:** 5 %, then 6.5 % (+1.5 points) from 2027-03-01, then 7.5 % (+1 point) from 2028-03-01.

## Not expressible (left out)

- § 4 stk. 2 farligt-gods-tillæg (6.25 kr. per hour, task-bound), § 11 rådighedstillæg, § 12 udetillæg and kostpenge, § 21 stk. 4 TR-tillæg, § 23 fund.
- Elever (72 % or 85 % of the base, and 3 % pension paid by the employer) and under-18s (98.40 kr.) are special groups. Their pension rule cannot sit beside the general one.

## Possible mistakes in the agreement

- None found. All percentages and kr. rises come out exact.
